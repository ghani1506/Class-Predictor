01

Purpose of this predictor

Explore Possible Future Pathways

This predictor is designed for Year 7 and Year 8 students. It helps students, parents and teachers explore the Year 9 programme that best matches the student's current academic profile.

Where all programme criteria are met, an exact match is shown. Otherwise, the predictor suggests the nearest programme based on the subject marks.

Important: This is an early awareness tool. It does not determine the student's final streaming outcome.

i

Important notice

For Awareness Purposes Only

This predictor checks for an exact programme match first. Where no exact match exists, it suggests the nearest programme using distances from the subject marks to the programme ranges.

A nearest-match prediction does not mean that all programme criteria have been met. The nearest-match method is an added recommendation rule and is not specified in the supplied channelling chart.

This is not a final streaming decision. Actual streaming may depend on later assessment results, student progress, school criteria and other relevant considerations.
