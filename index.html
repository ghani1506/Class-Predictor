Recommended Year 9 Stream

The recommendation is based on the programme with the strongest weighted match to the student's subject profile.

How the Recommendation is Calculated

English 2 points

Best 3 Non-Core 1 point each

Maximum weighted score: 12 points . The programme with the strongest overall match is recommended.

If the two strongest programmes are very close, the result will be identified as a borderline profile for further school consideration.

About this Predictor

  • Method: Weighted best-match streaming model.
  • Programmes: Programme 1, Programme 2, Programme 3, Programme 4 and Programme 5.
  • Core weighting: English, Mathematics and Science receive 2 points each.
  • Other compulsory subjects: Bahasa Melayu, MIB and IRK receive 1 point each.
  • Non-core subjects: The best 3 available scores are used. Arabic and Drama are optional.
  • Borderline profiles: Students whose two strongest programme scores are very close will be highlighted for further consideration.
  • Privacy: All calculations are performed locally in the browser.

This predictor is intended to support school streaming decisions. The final placement may also take account of teacher judgement, student progress and other relevant school information.
