About this Predictor

  • Method: Rule-based Year 9 streaming criteria.
  • Programmes: Programme 1, Programme 2, Programme 3, Programme 4 and Programme 5.
  • Non-core subjects: The best 3 scores from Social Studies, Arabic, Drama and BAT are used.
  • Privacy: All calculations are performed locally in the browser.

The result is intended to support the streaming process. Students with mixed score patterns that do not fit one complete programme band will be flagged for manual review.
