01

Purpose of this predictor

Explore Possible Future Pathways

This predictor is designed for Year 7 and Year 8 students. It helps students, parents and teachers gain early awareness of the Year 9 programme that currently best matches the student's academic profile.

Important: This is an early awareness tool. It does not determine the student's final streaming outcome.

Using the result

Guidance for Parents, Teachers & Students

P

For Parents

Use the result to understand the direction the student's current performance may be leading. Discuss strengths, areas for improvement and learning support with the student and school.

T

For Teachers

Use the prediction as an early monitoring indicator to identify students who may benefit from intervention, enrichment or closer progress tracking.

7

For Year 7 Students

The prediction provides an early indication of where current academic performance may lead, giving more time to strengthen weaker subjects.

8

For Year 8 Students

The prediction provides a more immediate indication of possible Year 9 streaming and can support preparation before the actual streaming process.

i

Important notice

For Awareness Purposes Only

This predictor provides an early indication of possible future streaming based on the student's current academic performance.

It does not represent a final streaming decision. Actual streaming may depend on later assessment results, student progress, school criteria and other relevant considerations.
